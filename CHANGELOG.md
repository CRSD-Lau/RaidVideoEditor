# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file. The format is
based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the
project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Repository publishing safeguards, pinned CI, and automated secret
  scanning.
- Repository social preview and consolidated documentation index.
- Explicit opt-in microphone retention for highlight review and portrait
  exports, with cache invalidation when the selected audio mix changes.
- A Friday shortcut that verifies the completed landscape recording, creates
  or safely reuses a dated config, prepares review lanes, and records local
  campaign evidence without rendering a final or publishing media.
- OBS dual-recording preflight, reusable countdown/ending browser assets, and
  separately bound Aitum portrait footage for highlight review and export.
- Exact local spoken `clip it` detection on isolated Discord and microphone
  stems, with explicit coverage states and protected review proposals.
- Optional local faster-whisper and Ollama editorial/vision analysis, timed
  evidence references, bounded setup/payoff refinement, provenance, explicit
  model setup, and optional environment-local GPU speech support.
- Explicit keep/maybe/reject feedback, stable candidate identity, rejection
  reasons, bounded advisory examples, and blinded baseline/current comparison.
- Social packages for Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Twitch
  Uploads, with reviewed copy, publication receipts, analytics, and retention holds.
- A consolidated growth workflow for zero to two Shorts, source lineage,
  factual claims, recap decisions, related-video targets/receipts, locked
  calendars, packaging experiments, production time, and dependency-aware holds.
- Synthetic and mocked regression coverage for speech/intelligence failure
  states, native media synchronization, source-bound review approval, and
  package/workflow safety.

### Changed

- Browser-facing pull and highlight reviews use VP9/Opus WebM and full proposed
  windows, with direct clip links and visible playback failure feedback.
- New Friday configs request native portrait video and local editorial
  intelligence. Existing dated configs and approvals remain unchanged; omitted
  video-source fields retain the legacy landscape behavior.
- Native video preserves the recorded Aitum composition and mixes the verified
  landscape audio stems. Separated shared-audio samples must establish a
  consistent offset and full candidate coverage; unusable native footage does
  not trigger silent landscape fallback.
- Review ratings remain separate from export approval. Changing the native
  source, timing, or audio binding requires a fresh review of that presentation.
- Highlight ranking no longer saturates at 1.0. Relative audio evidence,
  routine-kill limits, overlap suppression, and complete semantic abstention
  reduce unsupported or repetitive suggestions without imposing a quota.
- Public setup, workflow, licensing, security, and historical-evidence
  documentation now describe the maintained application and optional runtime.

### Fixed

- Player-death evidence now checks destination identity, excluding NPC/totem
  deaths and empty source GUIDs that previously inflated death-pressure signals.
- Weak volume/motion and kill/death combinations no longer establish Funny or
  Clutch labels by themselves.
- Partial/unavailable local analysis is reported distinctly from a complete
  empty result; required analysis fails before saving a proposal queue.
- Human trims clear excluded machine setup/payoff anchors and validate the
  editable peak. Feedback does not infer rejection from an unchecked proposal
  or multiply votes when the same decision is imported again.
- Review caches include relevant source, timing, format, and audio changes;
  failed native media QA preserves the prior completed clip and manifest.
- Final validation, render reuse, and YouTube upload now require matching final
  paths and full SHA-256 bindings rather than accepting stale or incomplete
  receipts. Growth imports cross-check social, render, and actual-media hashes,
  and approval rechecks file identity before recording the mutation.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-15

### Added

- Review-first OBS recording analysis and full winning-pull previews.
- Evidence-based ICC Normal and Heroic classification with scoreline titles.
- Game, Discord, motion, death, and kill-climax highlight proposals.
- Approval-gated 1440p60 masters and portrait social exports.
- Professional YouTube metadata, chapters, thumbnails, playlist management,
  publication confirmation, and retention reporting.
- Read-only OBS preflight and copy-only, SHA-256-verified archiving.
- Synthetic fixtures, unit tests, integration tests, and operational runbooks.
